My progress / orbs disappeared

Progress is stored locally on your device. Reinstalling the app, or restoring from a backup of a different device, can reset local progress. We're unable to recover locally stored data once it's cleared.

How do I turn off sound or haptics?

Open the in-game Settings menu from the main screen to toggle sound effects and haptic feedback.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Settings > Data > Reset All Game Progress inside the app.
